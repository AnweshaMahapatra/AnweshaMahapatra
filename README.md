# 👋 Welcome to My Data Playground

Most people see spreadsheets.
I see customer behaviour, business risks, missed opportunities, and stories waiting to be uncovered.

Somewhere between writing my first SQL query and building my first dashboard, I realized something:

**Good analysis doesn’t just describe a business, it explains what’s really happening underneath it.**

That idea became the foundation of every project you’ll find here.

This GitHub is a collection of business problems explored through:

* 📊 Power BI dashboards
* 🧠 Data storytelling
* 🗄️ SQL-driven analysis
* 📈 KPI and performance tracking
* 🔍 Real-world business case studies

From festive sales campaigns generating millions in incremental revenue…
to profit analysis exposing hidden losses behind high sales…
to workforce dashboards revealing attendance and WFH trends, every project here is built with one goal:

### Turning raw data into decisions people can actually use.

I enjoy working on projects where data is messy, business questions are unclear, and the answer isn’t obvious at first glance.

So if you like:

* dashboards that go beyond visuals,
* analytics that explain *why*,
* and projects that feel close to real business scenarios

You’re in the right place.

Take a look around.
